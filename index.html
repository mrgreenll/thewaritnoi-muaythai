The gym

A working Muay Thai camp, not a holiday class

Krabi Lion Muay Thai is the gym of Thewaritnoi s.k.v. Our fighters train here and compete on Krabi cards — and the same coaches hold pads for a first-timer on their first afternoon.

You get real coaching, not a scripted circuit. Someone watches your kick, corrects it, and makes you throw it again. Train once while you are passing through Ao Nang, or come every day for a month.

Whether you want to get fit or you are a fighter preparing to turn pro, the session is built around you. Kids train here too.

Training

What a round here
actually looks like

Warm-up, technique, then pads until the coach is satisfied. Clinch and sparring when you are ready for them — never before.

Pad work

One-to-one with a coach on the pads. Punches, elbows, knees and kicks, called in combinations until the shape is right. This is the core of every session.

Clinch & sparring

The part of Muay Thai you cannot learn from a video. Controlled clinch work and light sparring with partners at your level, supervised the whole way.

Bags & conditioning

Heavy bag rounds, skipping, bodyweight and core work. Where the fitness comes from — and where you can push at your own pace on a quiet afternoon.

Beach & rooftop

Sunrise drills on the sand with the karst islands behind, and pad rounds on the covered rooftop ring under the flags. Krabi is part of the training.
